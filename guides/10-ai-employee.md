# The AI employee

The AI employee is a chat assistant inside [Presora](https://presora.co). It answers from your live business data and prepares changes for you to confirm. It's on the Growth and Enterprise plans ([compare plans](https://presora.co/plans)).

## What it can do

- **Answer from live data:** your inbox, customers and pipeline, website pages, the setup checklist, and where everything is in Presora. It only uses what the person asking could open themselves.
- **Prepare actions as cards:** a reply to an email, a new email, a note on a customer, moving a customer to another stage. Nothing is sent or changed until you press the button (or say *"send it"*).
- **Manage automations:** *"make an automation that thanks people who fill in the quote form"*, *"wait 2 days instead of 1 in the review request"*, *"switch off the weekly digest"*.
- **Change wording on the website:** *"change 'Four steps. Zero surprises.' to 'Four key steps'"*. It shows the old text crossed out next to the new text.
- **Move a map:** *"we moved to 14 Mill Lane, Leeds"* or *"add Round Rock to the areas we cover"*, with a **Check on Google Maps** button.
- **Rewrite the home page** to answer what visitors asked but couldn't find (one AI credit, only when you ask).

**What it can't do:** change your plan or billing, connect a domain, invite teammates, or change anything your access doesn't allow. For those, it points you to the right page or to an admin.

**Today's briefing** is a written summary each morning. Past conversations are under **History** in the chat, and **New chat** starts a fresh one.

Official doc: [What the AI employee can do](https://presora.co/docs/assistant-abilities)

## Let it set up your business

Ask *"Let's set up my business"*, or ask for one change, like *"make the cart reminder 6 hours"*. You get a **plan card** showing each setting's before → after, and why.

- Ask for changes in your own words, and it shows a new version of the plan.
- **Nothing changes until you press Apply** on the card, or say a clear *"yes, apply it"*.
- Settings change one by one, and a snapshot is taken first. **Undo** on the card puts the earlier values back.
- A teammate can only apply changes in the areas their access allows. An admin can apply the rest.

A plan can cover automations and their timings, the blog autopilot, booking rules, the chatbot, and shop delivery, checkout and payments.

Official doc: [Let the AI employee set up the business](https://presora.co/docs/assistant-setup-plans)

## Prompts to try

- *"What came in this week that I haven't replied to?"*
- *"Draft a reply to the latest quote request with a price range of £300–£400."*
- *"Which qualified customers haven't heard from us in a week?"*
- *"Move Sarah Jones to Won and add a note that she paid the deposit."*
- *"Switch on the review request automation and wait 3 days instead of 2."*
- *"Where do I change my opening hours?"*

If it says you've used this week's allowance, see [The AI employee says I've used this week's allowance](https://presora.co/docs/assistant-allowance).
