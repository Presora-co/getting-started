# Get found: search engines and AI answers

People find local businesses through Google and, more and more, by asking AI assistants like ChatGPT. [Presora](https://presora.co) sites are built for both. Every client site gets an `llms.txt` file, a plain-text summary of the business that AI tools can read.

## Check where you stand

- **[Free website checker](https://presora.co/tools/website-checker):** checks any website (yours or a competitor's) for the basics that affect search and enquiries.
- **[Free llms.txt generator](https://presora.co/tools/llms-txt-generator):** makes an `llms.txt` for any site, including ones not on Presora.

## Quick wins on your Presora site

1. **Connect your own domain.** A branded domain builds trust and keeps your search history in one place. → [Domain and email](03-domain-and-email.md)
2. **Add area pages** for each town you cover. Local searches often include the town. → [Add, hide or remove pages](https://presora.co/docs/website-pages)
3. **Keep Business info complete and current.** Your address, hours and services feed the site, the chatbot and what AI tools read about you. → [Business info](https://presora.co/docs/business-info)
4. **Publish regularly.** The [blog autopilot](08-marketing-and-blog.md#the-blog-and-the-blog-autopilot) can post two articles a week on topics your customers search for.
5. **Answer the questions people actually ask.** Your chatbot's *Most-asked questions* list is a ready-made list of topics for FAQs and blog posts. → [Chatbot and leads](04-chatbot-and-leads.md)
6. **Fix the address on the map** if you've moved or cover new areas. → [Change the address or towns on your website's map](https://presora.co/docs/website-map)

More in the official docs under [Get found](https://presora.co/docs).
