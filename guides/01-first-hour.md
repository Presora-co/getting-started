# Your first hour on Presora

When you sign up, [Presora](https://presora.co) asks a few plain-language questions (about five minutes) and generates a multi-page website in a design made for your trade. This guide covers what to do next so the site, chatbot and forms all work from real information about your business.

## 1. Fill in Business info

**Business info** is the single place for your details. Everything else reads from it: the website, the chatbot, emails and the AI employee.

It has five sections:

- **Business**: name, type of business, services
- **Contact & location**: email, phone, address
- **More details**: your story, payment methods, SEO
- **Website content**: logo, photos of your work, opening hours and more
- **Domain & email**: your own domain

One **Save changes** saves every section. The chatbot uses the new details from its very next answer.

> **Tip:** Be specific about services and prices. "Boiler servicing from £80, within 15 miles of Leeds" lets the chatbot and the site answer real questions. "Heating services" doesn't.

Official doc: [Update your business details](https://presora.co/docs/business-info)

## 2. Build it into your website

Your name, contact details and brand colour update on the site by themselves. Sections built *from* Business info (hours, pricing, team, FAQs and so on) update when you press **Build into my website** on **Finish setup**.

- It updates only the sections it owns.
- It removes sections whose field you cleared.
- It never touches sections you added by hand.
- It's safe to run as often as you like, and it uses no AI credits.

Then press **Open editor** to check the result.

Official doc: [Put what you filled in on Business info onto the website](https://presora.co/docs/build-into-site)

## 3. Work through the Finish setup checklist

**Finish setup** lists what's still missing: logo, contact details, opening hours, photos, the chatbot and more. Each step links straight to the field. The setup reminder also sits under the bell until the essential steps are done.

## 4. Add your logo and photos

Upload them in **Business info → Logo** and **Photos of your work**, then run **Build into my website** again. You can also replace any image on a page by clicking it in the editor. Everything you upload is kept in the **Asset library**.

Official doc: [Add your logo and photos](https://presora.co/docs/logo-photos)

## 5. Decide what visitors can do

Pick the ones that fit your business:

- **Get in touch.** The contact form and Leads inbox are on every plan. → [Chatbot and leads](04-chatbot-and-leads.md)
- **Book.** Online booking for appointments, classes or tables. → [Online booking](06-booking.md)
- **Buy.** An online shop on your site. → [Online shop](09-online-shop.md)

## 6. Put it on your own domain

Your site already has a free `yourname.presora.co` address with HTTPS. When you're ready, connect your own domain. → [Domain and email](03-domain-and-email.md)

## Or let the AI employee do it

On plans with the AI employee, type *"Let's set up my business"*. It proposes a plan card showing each setting before and after and why, and nothing changes until you press **Apply**. → [The AI employee](10-ai-employee.md)
