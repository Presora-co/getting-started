# Customers and follow-ups

[Presora's CRM](https://presora.co/features/crm) keeps every customer in one pipeline, fed automatically by your website, forms and chatbot. The CRM is on the Growth and Enterprise plans ([compare plans](https://presora.co/plans)).

## The pipeline

**Customers** is a board: every customer is a card in a stage, **New → Contacted → Qualified → Won** (or **Lost**). Drag a card between columns, or use the dropdown.

- Leads from the website land there automatically.
- The menu says **Clients** for an appointments business and **Guests** for a restaurant, café or gym.
- The tiles at the top show *Customers*, *Open* (new, contacted or qualified), *Open value* and *Won value*.
- Values marked **✦ ~** are AI estimates from the lead's message. Open the customer to see why, and type your own number to replace it.
- **Segments:** tag customers, then filter the board by tag.

A contractor's Customers page is a list instead, and the pipeline lives in **Projects**. See [Projects: every job from enquiry to final payment](https://presora.co/docs/projects).

Official doc: [Your customers and sales pipeline](https://presora.co/docs/crm-pipeline)

> **Tip:** Moving cards matters. Several [pre-built automations](../automations/leads-and-enquiries.md) run on stage changes. Marking someone **Won** can trigger a review request, and leaving a lead in **New** for three days triggers a follow-up.

## A customer's page

Each customer has a timeline of everything that happened, plus notes, emails, tags and follow-ups. See [A customer's page](https://presora.co/docs/crm-customer-page). To bring in an existing list, see [Add, import or export customers](https://presora.co/docs/crm-add-import-export).

## Follow-ups

**Customers → Open follow-ups** has two kinds:

### Reminders (for you)

Add them on the Follow-ups page or on a customer's page. You get an email when one falls due, and they show on the **Calendar** too.

### Automatic follow-ups (for the customer)

Switch on **Send automatic follow-ups**, choose how many business days to wait (1–10), and write the subject and message. `{name}` and `{business}` are filled in for you. Only one follow-up is ever sent per conversation.

These track emails you send from inside Presora, so you need a Presora email address. See [Domain and email](03-domain-and-email.md).

### Reply to new leads with AI

**Automatically reply to new leads with AI** (on the same page) answers a customer's email straight away from your business info. Anything it can't answer confidently, like a quote, a booking or a complaint, is left for you. It sends at most one auto-reply per customer per day.

Official doc: [Follow-ups: reminders and automatic follow-up emails](https://presora.co/docs/follow-ups)
