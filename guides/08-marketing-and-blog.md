# Marketing and blog

Tools in [Presora](https://presora.co) for bringing people to your site and turning them into customers: ad landing pages, promotions, pop-ups, tracking pixels and an AI blog writer.

## Ad landing pages

A focused page for one ad campaign, with its own form. Leads from it are marked *From an ad* in **Leads**. See the [ad landing pages](https://presora.co/features/ad-landing-pages) feature page and [Make a landing page for an ad](https://presora.co/docs/landing-pages).

> **Pair it with:** [Email the landing page leads who qualify](../automations/leads-and-enquiries.md#email-the-landing-page-leads-who-qualify), which emails only the leads whose answer passes your filter.

## Promotions and pop-ups

- [Run a promotion or offer on your website](https://presora.co/docs/promotions), shown as a bar or a pop-up
- [Collect emails or phone numbers with a pop-up](https://presora.co/docs/popup-signups), optionally giving a discount code

Live promotions are added to what the chatbot knows automatically.

> **Pair it with:** the [pop-up and promotion automations](../automations/team-alerts-and-reports.md#promotions-and-pop-ups).

## Tracking pixels

**My website → Analytics & pixels:** paste your Google Analytics 4 measurement ID, Google Tag Manager container ID, Meta Pixel ID or TikTok Pixel ID, then **Save**.

- They load only after a visitor accepts cookies. The cookie notice appears on its own once a pixel is set.
- Shop sites also report product views, add to cart, checkout and purchases.

Official doc: [Add Google Analytics, Tag Manager, Meta or TikTok pixels](https://presora.co/docs/tracking-pixels)

## The blog and the blog autopilot

Write posts yourself ([Write and publish blog posts](https://presora.co/docs/blog)), or let the [AI blog writer](https://presora.co/features/blog-writer) plan and write them. The blog writer is on the Growth and Enterprise plans ([compare plans](https://presora.co/plans)).

**Blog → Autopilot:**

- **Posts per week:** up to five. Two is a good pace.
- **Days and time**, **post length** (Short ~600, Medium ~1,100, Long ~1,800 words), and **what it should cover**.
- It plans about 20 topics ahead on an editable calendar. You can edit, move, write now or skip each one.
- It researches each topic on the web on the day, then writes it.

**When a post is written**, choose one:

- **Publish it automatically**: written about an hour before its slot and published on time.
- **Wait for my approval**: written a day ahead as a draft, and it goes live once you approve.

> **Tip:** Start with **Wait for my approval** for the first few weeks. Once you trust the tone, switch to automatic publishing.

Official docs: [Let the blog autopilot plan and write posts](https://presora.co/docs/blog-autopilot) · [Comments on blog posts](https://presora.co/docs/blog-comments)

> **Pair it with:** the [blog automations](../automations/team-alerts-and-reports.md#blog), which alert the team when a post is ready to review, when one goes live, and when someone comments.
