# Online booking

[Presora's online booking](https://presora.co/features/online-booking) lets visitors book appointments, classes, tables or calls straight from your website. It's on every plan. Industry examples: [hair salons](https://presora.co/for/hair-salons), [dentists](https://presora.co/for/dentists), [personal trainers](https://presora.co/for/personal-trainers), [restaurants](https://presora.co/for/restaurants), [gyms](https://presora.co/for/gyms).

## 1. Switch booking on and set availability

**Booking settings:** tick *"Take bookings — show the schedule-a-call form to visitors"*.

Then set:

- **Your timezone**
- **Call length** (15–120 minutes)
- **Minimum notice** (hours)
- **How far ahead to offer** (days, up to 90)
- **Bookings at the same time**: how many people can book the same start time, like your tables or the places in a class (1 means one at a time)
- **Weekly hours** and **Days you're busy** (holidays and days off), then **Save availability**

Official doc: [Booking settings: when people can book](https://presora.co/docs/booking-settings)

> **Tip:** Opening hours (shown on the website) and booking hours (when people can book) are separate settings. If you change one, check the other. See [Opening hours and booking hours](https://presora.co/docs/booking-hours).

## 2. Add services and team

**Services & team** (called *What guests can book* for a restaurant, café or gym): list your services with category, name, description, price and length. Add your team with the services each person does and their own hours. Clients pick a service, then a person.

Official doc: [Services & team](https://presora.co/docs/services-team)

## 3. Put the calendar on your site

In the website editor, add the **Booking calendar** block (under *Commerce*). It uses your availability automatically.

## 4. Manage bookings

- [Your appointments diary](https://presora.co/docs/appointments-diary)
- [Reservations for a restaurant, café or gym](https://presora.co/docs/reservations)
- [Bookings for other kinds of business (call scheduler)](https://presora.co/docs/bookings-page)
- [Cancel a booking or reservation](https://presora.co/docs/cancel-booking)
- [Calendar: booked calls and follow-ups in one place](https://presora.co/docs/calendar)
- [Booking confirmation and reminder emails](https://presora.co/docs/booking-emails)

Bookings made on the website also show up in **Leads** and in your inbox.

## Cut no-shows

The [day-before reminder automation](../automations/bookings.md) is on by default. It thanks people as soon as they book and emails a reminder 24 hours before.
