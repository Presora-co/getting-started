# Online shop

A [Presora](https://presora.co) website can sell products, with a catalogue, cart, customer accounts, wishlists, orders and discount codes. Examples: [local shops](https://presora.co/for/shops), [clothing brands](https://presora.co/for/clothing-brands), [florists](https://presora.co/for/florists), [custom print shops](https://presora.co/for/custom-print-shops).

> **Payments:** the shop takes **cash on delivery, bank transfer, or payment in the shop**. There are no card payments.

## Turn the shop on

The shop lives on your website, so you need a website first.

1. Open **Shop** and press **Turn the shop on**.
2. A Shop page is added to your site's menu, along with the cart, checkout, account and wishlist pages.
3. Add products, or import them.

You then get a **Shop** area with Overview, Orders, Sales attribution, Products, Categories & brands, Shop customers, Reviews, Discount codes and Shop settings. If your business type is an online store, Orders, Products and Customers sit at the top of the menu instead.

Official doc: [Open an online shop on your website](https://presora.co/docs/shop-open)

## Shop automations

A shop comes with its own ready-made automations: cart reminders, back-in-stock and price-drop emails, review requests, low-stock alerts, a Monday sales summary and more. See **[Online shop automations](../automations/online-shop.md)** and the official doc [The shop's ready-made automations](https://presora.co/docs/shop-automations).

> **Tip:** Mark orders **completed** promptly. The order survey and review-request automations count their days from that moment.
