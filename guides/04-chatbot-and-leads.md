# Chatbot and leads

Every [Presora](https://presora.co) website comes with an [AI support chatbot](https://presora.co/features/ai-chatbot) that answers visitors from your own business information and turns interested visitors into leads. Not sure you need one? Read [Does a small business need a website chatbot?](https://presora.co/blog/does-my-business-need-a-website-chatbot)

## Tell the chatbot what it knows

**Support chatbot → Assistant settings → What your assistant knows.** Put in everything it may tell customers: services, prices, hours, delivery or service areas, and policies.

- Leave it empty and the chatbot uses a summary built from Business info.
- **Start from my business info** pre-fills it so you can edit from there.
- Live promotions are added to what it knows automatically.

In the same place, set the **Opening message** and **Suggested questions** (one per line), and use the on/off switch. **Save assistant settings** takes effect from the next question. There's nothing to rebuild.

> **Tip:** Write it the way you'd brief a new receptionist: "We don't do same-day call-outs on Sundays. Quotes are free within 10 miles. We take card and bank transfer."

Official doc: [Set up your website chatbot](https://presora.co/docs/support-chatbot)

## Fill the gaps every week

**Support chatbot** shows **What it couldn't answer** and **Most-asked questions** for the last 14 days. Add those answers to *What your assistant knows* (or to your website), and it answers them from then on.

Want this to come to you? Switch on the [unanswered-questions automation](../automations/chatbot.md#hear-about-questions-your-chatbot-couldnt-answer). It emails you each one with an AI-suggested answer.

**Weekly allowance:** each plan includes a number of answered questions a week ([see plans](https://presora.co/plans)). **Questions answered this week** shows where you are. When the allowance runs out, the chatbot points visitors to your contact email until the week resets.

Official doc: [Questions the chatbot couldn't answer](https://presora.co/docs/chatbot-unanswered)

## How the chatbot captures leads

When a visitor sounds ready to book or buy, or needs a person to follow up, the chatbot asks for their phone number or email in its own words. A box under the reply lets them leave it in one tap (**Call me back** or **Send**). Whatever they leave arrives as **one lead in your CRM with the conversation attached**, and you're notified the first time.

**Conversations** lists every question and exactly what the chatbot replied, and it's searchable.

## The Leads inbox

**Leads** lists everything people asked for through your website: messages, quote requests, appointments, callbacks, reservations and chatbot leads. Filter with the tabs (*All, Messages, Quotes, Appointments, Callbacks, Reservations, From chat*). A restaurant, café or gym sees this as **Enquiries**.

Each lead shows:

- what they sent (and, for chat leads, what they asked)
- what it's likely worth: the budget they gave, or else the AI's estimate
- **Reply by email**, **Call** and **View customer**
- a *From an ad* label if it came through an [ad landing page](https://presora.co/features/ad-landing-pages)

Official doc: [Leads: everything people asked for on your website](https://presora.co/docs/leads)
