# Edit your website

Every [Presora](https://presora.co) website can be changed in two ways: describe the change to the AI in plain English, or click and edit by hand. Both happen in the same editor. See the [AI website builder](https://presora.co/features/ai-website-builder) feature page for an overview.

## Open the editor

**My website → Edit & AI.** The AI assistant chat is always open on the left, with the preview beside it. **Edit by hand** (top right) opens the panel for sections, colours, the site frame and History.

**Save to live site** publishes immediately. There's no separate publish step.

## Edit by hand

- Click any text in the preview to edit it.
- Click an image or background to replace it.
- Click a section to see a small bar with **Ask AI** and **Edit settings**.
- **＋ Add a section** adds one. Drag sections to reorder them.
- Theme colour and font apply to every page.
- Switch between the **Desktop** and **Mobile** preview before you save.

## Ask the AI

Type a request into *"Describe a change, or ask a question…"*. The AI edits the right page, adds a page if your request needs one, and remembers the last few messages, so *"make it shorter"* follows on from the previous change.

Requests that work well:

- *"Add a section about our 24-hour emergency call-outs under the hero."*
- *"Make the services page sound friendlier and cut it by a third."*
- *"Add a page for our Leeds and Bradford service areas."*
- *"Replace the hero picture with a photo of a finished kitchen."*

**Be precise with Select.** Under the message box, **Select** lets you drag a box over part of the preview, or click one spot. The sections and text under it go with your next message, so the AI changes exactly those.

**Pictures.** Describe the picture you want, for example *"a photo with only pizza in it"*. The AI picks from your uploads and Presora's stock photos for your kind of business. If nothing fits, it tells you what to upload, and no credit is used.

Official doc: [Ask the AI to change your website](https://presora.co/docs/ai-website-edits)

## AI credits

- Each AI change costs **1 AI credit**.
- Questions in the chat (*"how do I connect my domain?"*) are free.
- The same balance pays for AI-written landing pages, *Fix this with AI* in SEO, and AI changes to automations.
- Credits refill with your plan each month, and some plans have unlimited credits. See [plans](https://presora.co/plans).
- **Out of credits?** Editing by hand, asking questions and restoring old versions are all still free.

## Undo anything

Every saved edit is kept in **History**, below the preview on **My website** and in the editor. **Restore** puts an earlier version back on the live site. Restoring is free, and the list keeps the 25 most recent versions.

Official doc: [Undo a website change or restore an earlier version](https://presora.co/docs/website-history)
