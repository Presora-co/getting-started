# Automations for the chatbot

Workflows built around the [Presora AI chatbot](https://presora.co/features/ai-chatbot). Set the chatbot up first: [Chatbot and leads guide](../guides/04-chatbot-and-leads.md).

---

## Hear about questions your chatbot couldn't answer

**Status:** On by default · **Sends:** an email to you

> A chatbot that gets smarter every week.

**When:** the chatbot can't answer a visitor's question.

**What happens:**

1. AI suggests an answer, based on what it knows about your business.
2. You're emailed the question with the suggested answer.

**Then:** paste the answer (corrected if needed) into **Support chatbot → What your assistant knows**. The chatbot answers that question from then on.

**Tip:** If you'd rather not get one email per question, switch this off and check **What it couldn't answer** on the Support chatbot page once a week instead.

---

## Fast-track chatbot leads

**Status:** Optional · **Sends:** an email to you

> Call warm leads while they're still warm.

**When:** the chatbot captures someone's phone number or email.

**What happens:**

1. Marks them **Qualified** in your pipeline and tags them `chatbot`.
2. Creates a call task for today, with the chat conversation attached.
3. Emails you.

**Why:** someone who asked the chatbot questions and then left their number is usually ready to buy. A call within the hour beats an email the next day.
