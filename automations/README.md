# Pre-built automations for Presora

Ready-made workflows you can switch on in [Presora](https://presora.co) in a minute or two. Each recipe below says what it does, when it runs, what it sends, and how to adjust it.

New to automations? Read the [Automations guide](../guides/07-automations.md) first, or see the [automations feature page](https://presora.co/features/automations). Automations are on the Growth and Enterprise plans ([compare plans](https://presora.co/plans)).

## Where to find them

Open **Automations** in your Presora dashboard. Every recipe here appears there in one of three ways:

| Label in this repo | Where it is in Presora | Starts as |
|---|---|---|
| **On by default** | *Presora defaults → On for everyone* | Already running. Switch it off if you don't want it. |
| **Optional** | *Presora defaults → Switch on if you want them* | Ready, but off until you switch it on. |
| **Recipe** | *Start from a recipe* | Opens in the editor, switched off, for you to check. |

**Sends on its own** means the workflow emails the customer directly. **Leaves a draft** means the email waits in **Automations → Waiting for you** until you read and send it.

## Good first picks

If you only switch on three:

1. **Follow up when a lead goes quiet**, so nobody who enquired is forgotten.
2. **Remind people the day before their booking**, if you take bookings.
3. **Ask happy customers for a review**, because reviews win the next customer.
