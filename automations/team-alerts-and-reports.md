# Team alerts and reports

Workflows that keep you and your team informed by phone notification and/or email, so nothing important sits unread in [Presora](https://presora.co). "The team" means everyone on your Presora account.

---

## Reports

### Monday summary of your week

**Status:** On by default · **Sends:** a note to you

> Know how the website is doing without opening it.

**When:** every Monday at 8:00.

**What happens:** a note on your phone and in your inbox covering the last seven days: enquiries, bookings, chatbot conversations and deals won.

Running a shop? See the [Monday sales summary](online-shop.md#monday-sales-summary).

## Inbox and forms

### Flag urgent emails

**Status:** Optional · **Sends:** an email to you

> Never miss the email that can't wait.

**When:** any email reaches your Presora inbox.

**What happens:** AI reads it. Anything that needs you today, like a complaint, a deadline or an order problem, is emailed to you straight away with a one-line summary.

### Tell the team about every form that isn't a lead

**Status:** On by default · **Sends:** a note to the team

> No application sits unread in a list nobody checks.

**When:** someone submits a custom form that doesn't add them to your customer list, such as a job application.

**What happens:** everyone on the team gets a note on their phone and in their inbox with every answer and any attached files.

### Alert on an unhappy survey answer

**Status:** Optional · **Sends:** a note to the team

> An unhappy customer gets a call, not silence.

**When:** someone answers a survey with 1–3 stars, or 0–8 out of 10.

**What happens:** everyone on the team hears about it by phone and email, with every answer, so someone can call them today.

## Promotions and pop-ups

### Tell the team when a promotion ends

**Status:** Optional · **Sends:** a note to the team

> No stale offer, and no gap between offers nobody noticed.

**When:** a website promotion's end date passes, or someone switches it off.

### Tell the team about pop-up signups

**Status:** Optional · **Sends:** a note to the team

> No signup sits unnoticed.

**When:** someone leaves their email or phone number in a website pop-up. If it's a phone number, call or message them while they're still interested.

### Welcome email for pop-up signups

**Status:** Optional · **Sends:** on its own

> A first hello while they still remember signing up.

**When:** someone leaves their **email** in a website pop-up.

**What happens:** sends a short welcome straight away. If the promotion gives a discount code, the pop-up emails the code itself, so you don't need to add it here.

Set up promotions: [Run a promotion](https://presora.co/docs/promotions) · [Pop-up signups](https://presora.co/docs/popup-signups)

## Blog

These go with the [blog autopilot](../guides/08-marketing-and-blog.md#the-blog-and-the-blog-autopilot).

### A blog post is ready for review

**Status:** On by default · **Sends:** a note to the team

> No post misses its slot waiting for a yes.

**When:** the autopilot is set to *Wait for my approval* and a new post has been written. The note includes the title and when it's planned to go live.

### Tell the team when the autopilot publishes

**Status:** Optional · **Sends:** a note to the team

> Every new post gets read and shared.

**When:** the autopilot puts a new post live. The note includes the link, so you can share it.

### Tell the team about new blog comments

**Status:** On by default · **Sends:** a note to the team

> Every comment gets an answer while the reader is still there.

**When:** someone comments on a blog post. The note includes the comment and whether it's waiting for your approval.
