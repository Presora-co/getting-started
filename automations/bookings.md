# Automations for bookings

For businesses taking appointments, classes or tables through [Presora's online booking](https://presora.co/features/online-booking). Set booking up first: [Online booking guide](../guides/06-booking.md).

---

## Remind people the day before their booking

**Status:** On by default · **Sends:** on its own

> Fewer no-shows.

**When:** someone books on your website.

**What happens:**

1. Emails them a thank-you straight away.
2. Waits until **24 hours before** the appointment.
3. Emails them a reminder.

**Adjust it:**

- Change *24 hours* to *2 hours* for walk-in-style businesses (barbers, quick services), or add a second reminder.
- Edit the wording of both emails to include parking instructions, what to bring, or your cancellation policy.

---

## Ideas to build on top

Use [Describe an automation](build-your-own.md) to create these:

- *"When someone books, add a task for me to prepare the room an hour before."*
- *"Two days after an appointment, email the client a link to rebook."*
- *"When a booking is made, tag the customer with the service they booked."*
